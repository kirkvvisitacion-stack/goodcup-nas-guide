Good Cup

Get into the Good Cup NAS in 4 steps.

The NAS is our shared file box at the Parañaque HQ. Every farm photo, video, product shot, brand file and campaign lives in it, and you open it from your phone or laptop.

NAS means Network Attached Storage: hard drives plugged into the office internet, so the whole team can reach them.
  1. 1

    Open the invite and sign up

    Tap the button, choose Sign up, and use your Good Cup email, or the email you already use for Good Cup work.

    The invite is a private link for making your own login. Sign up means making that login, one time only. It opens in your browser (Safari, Chrome), so there is nothing to install.
    Open the invite
  2. 2

    Verify your email

    Open the email it sends you and click the link inside. If it is not there after a minute, check your spam folder.

    Verify means proving the email is really yours. Spam is the folder where your email app puts messages it is unsure about.
  3. 3

    Message Kirk on Telegram

    Send Kirk the email you used. This is the step people miss. You get no access until he gives you your folders.

    Kirk is the admin: the person who looks after the NAS and decides who sees which folders, based on your role. Telegram is the chat app we use at work, and @kirkvee is his username there.
    Message @kirkvee
  4. 4

    Open GoodNAS and sign in

    Once Kirk says you are in, open GoodNAS, sign in, then browse and download what you need. The same link works at the office, at home or on the road.

    GoodNAS is the name of our NAS login page, goodcup.aar6.ug.link. Download means saving a copy to your own phone or computer.
    Open GoodNAS

What is the NAS?

Think of Google Drive, except Good Cup owns it, it is private, and there is no monthly fee.

Where it lives
At the Parañaque HQ, switched on all day. You never need to go there: GoodNAS reaches it from anywhere.
The machine
A UGREEN DXP4800 Plus. UGREEN is the brand that makes it.
4 drive bays
Slots that each hold one hard drive, numbered 01 to 04. It keeps a safe copy, so one drive failing does not lose any work.
14.8 TB
Its total space. TB means terabyte, about 1,000 GB, which is room for years of footage.
What you see
Only the folders Kirk gives you, based on your role.
App
Not needed. A UGREEN app exists if you want one, but the browser does everything.
Stuck on a step? Message @kirkvee on Telegram.
