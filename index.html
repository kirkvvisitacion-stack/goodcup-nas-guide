Open the inviteSee the 4 steps
  1. 1

    Open the invite and sign up

    Tap Open the invite, choose Sign up, and use your Good Cup email, or the email you already use for Good Cup work. It opens in your browser, so there is nothing to install.

    The invite is a standing link for making your own login. It never expires, and Kirk approves each person. Sign up means making that login, one time only.
    Open the invite
  2. 2

    Verify your email

    Open the email it sends you and click the link inside. If it is not there after a minute, check your spam folder.

    Verify means proving the email is really yours. Spam is the folder where your email app puts messages it is unsure about.
  3. 3

    Message Kirk on Telegram

    Send Kirk the email you used. This is the step people miss. You get no access until he gives you your folders.

    Kirk is the admin: the person who looks after the NAS and decides who sees which folders, based on your role. Telegram is the chat app we use at work, and @kirkvee is his username there. No Telegram? Message Kirk the way you usually do.
    Message @kirkvee
  4. 4

    Open GoodNAS and sign in

    Once Kirk says you are in, open GoodNAS, sign in, then browse and download what you need. The same link works at the office, at home or on the road.

    Not sure where a file is? Use the NAS file search below, then follow its path in GoodNAS. GoodNAS is the name of our NAS login page, goodcup.aar3.ug.link. Download means saving a copy to your own phone or computer.
    Open GoodNAS

What is the NAS?

Like Google Drive, but owned by Good Cup.

Google Drive

  • Owned by another company
  • A fee every month
  • Files sit on another company's computers

Our NAS

  • Owned by Good Cup
  • No monthly fee
  • Files sit in our own box
Where it lives
NAS means Network Attached Storage: hard drives plugged into the office internet, so the whole team can reach them. Ours is at the Parañaque HQ, switched on all day.
4 drive bays
Made by UGREEN. Slots that each hold one hard drive, numbered 01 to 04. It keeps a safe copy, so one drive failing does not lose any work.
14.8 TB
Its total space. TB means terabyte, a very large amount of space, room for years of footage.
App
Not needed. A UGREEN app exists if you want one, but the browser does everything.

Why we have one

  • Everything in one place

    Farm trips, products, cafe and campaigns in clear folders, with no hunting across laptops.

  • Private and owned

    It belongs to Good Cup, and you only see the folders meant for your role.

  • Always on

    Open it at the office, at home or on the road through the same link.
