What is a NAS

First, the simple version

What is a NAS?

NAS stands for Network Attached Storage. That sounds technical, so here is the plain version.

It is like Google Drive, except we own it, it is private, and it lives right in our own office.

A cloud drive

Google Drive / Dropbox

  • Your files live on someone else's computers
  • You pay every month, and space runs out fast with video
  • Someone else sets the rules
Our NAS

The Good Cup NAS

  • Files live on a drive we own, in the Ramos Office
  • No monthly bill, and room for years of footage
  • Private to Good Cup, and we make the rules

Why we have one

One safe home for all our work.

Photos from the farms. Finished videos. Brand files. Instead of scattered laptops, hard drives, and cloud logins, it all lives in one place everyone can reach.

Everything in one place

Good to know

Quick questions.

Is my access safe and private?
Yes. The drive is owned by Good Cup and stays in our office. You reach it over a private, secure connection, and you only see the folders you are given access to.
Do I need to install an app?
No. The link opens in any web browser on your phone or laptop. There is a UGREEN app if you want it, but the browser is all you need to start.
What email should I use to register?
Use your Good Cup email if you have one. If you do not, use the email you already use for anything Good Cup, so the admin can recognize you and grant the right access.
What can I actually see once I am in?
It depends on your department and role. The admin sets your access when you give a heads-up in step three, so the admin can grant you the folders you need. If you need a folder you cannot see, just ask.
Can I use it when I am not in the office?
Yes. It works remotely through the same link, so you can grab files from home or on the road.
